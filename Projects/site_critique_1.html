Web Site Critiques

Cruitique 1: Pitchfork - https://pitchfork.com/

  1. My first impression was one of trying to tell what I was looking at. There was a lot of text and lines on the screen, so I had to stare blankly for just a second to become oriented. After this it was fairly easy to navigate the site and understand what was going on.
  2. The site is farirly organized. It has news at the top, and album reviews below this. However, I did have to read a lot to figure this out. The site has multiple columns of different sizes with news feeds. This makes finding a specific topic a little confusing, but not impossible. I also think that there is too much on the home page, making users scroll a lot. it would be better to just separate the information into separate pages through the menu at the top.
  3. The site structure does make sense. Even though I'm not a huge fan of it, it is not overly complicated or disorganized. They have news articles and album reviews on the home page, and more content below this.
  4. In my opinion, the graphics detract form the site. some of the graphics are overly busy and are animated. I ws only able to understand what the graphic meant after I had read the title that it corresponded with. I believe that graphics should facilitate understanding, and not the opposite. Additionally, not every section included pictures, and the picture sizing was somewhat inconsistent throughout sections.
  5. The page loaded reasonably fast, and all the content on the home page was available upon the site laoding.
  6. Technically, there was nothig wrong with text color and size. Black text on a white background is fairly normal. However, there was a lot og tet taht was either bolted, italicized, or both. This combination on a stark white background made me think that my eyes were shaking since a large portion of the site looked kind of fuzzy. Perhaps using a softer grey or less bolded text would help with this.
  7. The site allowed for easy navigation. There was a navigation bar that would appear and disappear so as to not block the view of the website. No issues here.
  8. The color scheme is largely black-and-white, and this is repeated on every page. In terms of layout, each page was fairly different. It wasn't a large enough difference to feel disorienting, but it wasn't incredibly intuitive.
  9. The site does respond well to smaller window sizes and shapes. Formatting doesn't feel cluky or quished on phone screens or smaller tabs.
  10. There was one part of the site that cought my eye. In the "new reader reviews" section of the page, the format for entries was different, akin to social media listing the user and post time. It felt like a distinctly different part of the site and like something that is not seen frequently.
